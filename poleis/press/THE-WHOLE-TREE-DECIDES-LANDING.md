## The attacks, answered

- **Who decides alone, after the ratification?** Nobody: every act of root 0 needs two of three agents in unit 1 AND more than half of the floor's active
  weight; the agents alone and the people alone each end split in both rehearsals.
- **His keys?** Nowhere. His own Lyssajudron is one seat, minted like anyone's.
- **A stranger with no permit?** Cannot mint, so cannot propose or vote; it can crank what anyone may send, and buy a seat from its owner.
- **A buyer with a permit?** One seat, bound to that buyer, this chain, this curve, its metadata, a price ceiling and an expiry; then one hexagon.
- **The issuer** (the mint door's permit key) decides alone who enters the floor while the window runs: "one hexagon a person" rests on its own check, and
  the chain holds only one seat a permit; it could seat many wallets of one hand, or sign nothing so root 0 never ratifies. Its key is fixed at the
  founding and has no power once the window closes.
- **A small floor:** if one seat is minted before the window closes, its owner is the whole floor; a seat silent past Poleis's activity window leaves the
  active weight, so at a seal past the deadline a small active part carries the floor.
- **Who blocks:** two of the three agents, or holders of half the floor's weight; two agent keys lost freeze the tree, with no road to reseat a chair
  without both chambers.
- **The deployer, before the ratification:** Poleis lets the founding's payer hang units on empty chairs while root 0 is in genesis; the press never does,
  and once its record holds a verified founding it does not read the chairs again. That key also holds the three programs' code until the handoff: its
  theft is total whatever the chairs say, so the handoff, not a second read, is what ends that power.
- **A crank:** lands only what it is handed and seals when the chain lets it; it chooses timing and pays fees, and cannot forge a vote or change what executes.
