## What the press does now

**The first sitting** (`press sit`: deploy, found, vault, warm, market, floor, acts):

- `found`: root 0 in the deploy's breath, in ONE transaction the deployer pays: `init_config`, `open_genesis` with no founder, and
  `found_child` of his posiverse as unit 1 in the chair `posiverse.chair` names, its chairs `posiverse.agents` in order (one to twelve,
  distinct, none of them a Keystone), its charter, window and plan its own. A standing root is held to all of it (`chain::founding_held`):
  a key in any chair of root 0, unit 1 in another chair, another unit in its chair, another crew, another order, a floor on unit 1, another
  parent, charter, window, plan or name, each refuses the root as a stranger's.
- `vault`: root 0's vault of the dollar by Poleis's permissionless `create_vault`; `warm` names it the mine's treasury, derived, never read
  from the params.
- `floor`: for each Keystone, the three transactions it signs alone: its sealed bid's commit, its reveal and its claim of one seat, at the
  reserve unless `genesis.bid` names more (never past the deposit). The salts are the press's, from the system's randomness, kept mode 600
  with the sets that carry them; a bid that stands under another commitment is refused.
- `acts`: the four acts' sets: Keystone 1's proposal, the one transaction; then the envelopes, one for each agent in unit 1 and one for each
  Keystone on root 0's floor.

**Between the sittings**, by anyone, the deployer paying: `press ratify` (the sale settled once its reveal window has passed, root 0 ratified
once a seat is claimed) and `press crank --signatures F` (`F` the signatures handed back, `{act, unit, voter, signature}`): for each act, in
Poleis's order and only what the chain takes now, unit 1 convened (an `ACT_RAW` act; a CODEX act opens unit 1's vote with its proposal, its
impacted set closed over the child), the agents' ballots into unit 1, the Keystones' onto root 0's floor, unit 1's seal, its `mandate`,
root 0's seal, and the execute, an act only after the ones before it. What stands is read and skipped, so a crank cut anywhere runs again;
a ballot of a voter with no voice in its chamber is named and never sent.

**The second sitting**, last, as before: `press handoff` once root 0 is a council and the fund constituted, now also only while root 0's
floor is his Keystones' alone (below); then `press certificate`.

## What the review changed

1. **The chair is a param.** The brief put unit 1 in chair 0. His Nov 20 answer puts his posiverse in the house the site draws at index 2:
   `posiverse.chair`, 2 on both files, read by the checks from the params, never a literal. Poleis takes the gap: `found_unit` asks only a
   chair under twelve and empty, the quorum counts lit chairs by bit (one lit, a quorum of one), and `convene` and `mandate` name the chair by
   the unit's own mask. A root whose unit 1 sits in another chair is refused (a fault of its own in `tests/root.rs`).
2. **The first floor is his hand and nobody else's.** Poleis's genesis sale is open: the top revealed bids win and all pay the next price
   down, floored at the reserve, so while the rung has seats to spare (twenty at rung 1, his Keystones take three) anyone takes a seat beside
   his at the reserve. With one such seat, two of his three are no longer more than half the floor (two of four), so every act needs all
   three; with as many as his, all three are not enough. The builder's own attack named it; nothing read for it. Now `press ratify` reads the sale's board once it is final and sends
   nothing while a bidder on it is none of his Keystones (`chain::board_is_his`), and `press handoff` reads the board and the floor at the
   moment the code would leave the deployer, before his finger is asked (`chain::floor_is_his`: every seat a Keystone's, each of the three
   holding one). It prices nothing and closes nothing on the chain: what a stranger's seat costs him is a founding made again, while the
   deployer still holds the code. The floor no stranger can enter is Poleis's other birth (below, "What is not done", 3).
3. **Tests that said what the code says.** The root tests built their good founding with `root_chairs` and `crew_chairs`, the functions the
   founding is held to, so a fault in either passed unseen (put in on purpose: unit 1 in chair 0 everywhere passed every root test). They are
   now said chair by chair. `vault_held` had no test: a vault answering to a wallet, another mint, another program, another length, each
   refused.

## The attacks, answered

- **His Keystones alone?** No: the chair is unit 1's and none of his keys sits in it (fault b); his envelope in unit 1 lands nothing.
- **The agents alone?** No: root 0 needs more than half of its floor's weight as well (fault a).
- **One key lost, in either chamber?** Two of three still pass.
- **Two keys lost in one chamber?** Every act stops, and the chairs cannot be replaced, since replacing them needs both chambers. Liveness,
  not safety: named in "What is not done", 4.
- **A stranger's seat in the genesis sale?** It can be bought; the press ratifies nothing and hands nothing while it stands (above, 2).
- **A stranger's root 0 first?** Caught by the read-back, as before (its authority is not the deployer, or its chairs are not these), and
  nothing is handed over.
- **A crank that is not the press?** It can land only envelopes already signed and send seals when they are due: it chooses timing and pays
  the fees, and cannot forge a vote or change what executes (the checks themselves cranked the faults, the market, the mine and the face).
- **The fund's chair quorum of 7 against root 0's one lit chair?** Different chairs: it gates the fund's own choice of an option by the
  fund's chairs, roots seated by their fees (`ix/fund.rs`, `judge`), not root 0's. With one root, no option is chosen until seven roots hold
  fund chairs. The launch plan's value, unchanged here.
- **Does anyone sign more than this?** The sets hold Keystone 1's proposals and one envelope a voter an act, nothing else; the crank holds only
  signatures it is handed; the deployer pays.

3. **The open sale.** A stranger's seat is refused at the ratification and the handoff, not prevented. The floor no stranger enters is the
   other birth Poleis carries: a root born by `open_nft_genesis`, whose seats are minted by one issuer's permit and vote by their owner's
   own transaction (which the Keystone page signs as it stands); the client builds none of its calls and no test walks `propose_nft` or
   `attest_nft` to a seal. Which birth root 0 takes is settled before a mainnet sitting.
4. **Liveness.** Two keys lost in one chamber freeze the swarm; a road that reseats a chair or a seat without both chambers is not built and
   would have to be weighed against "nobody can kill anybody".
